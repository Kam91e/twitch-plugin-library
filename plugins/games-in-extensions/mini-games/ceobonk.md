# CEOBONK

> Give your viewers a playful animated hammer cursor to bonk the stream screen, plus two bubble-popping mini-games powered by Bits.

## Quick Start

1. Go to the [Twitch Extension Dashboard](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/7jxiznd01xfopo19fx8itnoe9gpeln) and search for "CEOBONK".
2. Click **Install** and activate the extension as an **Overlay** or **Component** slot on your channel.
3. *(Optional — for mini-games)* Enable Bits for the extension under **Monetisation** > **Extension Manager** in your Twitch Creator Dashboard.
4. No OBS browser source is needed — CEOBONK runs natively in Twitch's extension system and loads automatically for all viewers on your channel.
5. Viewers see the animated hammer cursor immediately — no setup required on their end.

## Features

- **Hammer cursor overlay** — every viewer's cursor changes to an animated hammer when hovering over the stream.
- **Bonk interaction** — viewers can click/bonk anywhere on the screen for a satisfying visual and audio response.
- **Two bubble-popping mini-games** — activated via Bits (version 2.0.1+). Viewers spend Bits to pop bubbles live on stream.
- **Volume controls** — adjust the bonk sound effect volume directly from the overlay.
- **Display toggle** — show or hide the hammer cursor from the settings panel.

## Configuration

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---------|------|---------|-------------|
| Display Toggle | toggle | `on` | Shows or hides the hammer cursor overlay completely |
| Volume Slider | range 0–100 | `50` | Controls the volume of the bonk sound effect |

### Display Toggle
If you or your viewers find the animated cursor distracting during gameplay or quiet moments, you can hide it entirely from the overlay settings. Viewers will still see the hammer the next time they hover after re-enabling it.

### Volume Slider
The bonk sound plays each time a viewer clicks the screen. Adjust the volume from silent (0) to full (100) depending on your stream's audio mix. This setting is accessible via the overlay panel — no need to exit full-screen or open OBS.

### Bits Integration (Mini-Games)
To unlock the two bubble-popping mini-games, Bits must be enabled for the extension. Go to **Creator Dashboard** > **Monetisation** > **Extension Manager** and toggle on Bits for CEOBONK. Streamers earn 80 % of every Bit spent on the mini-games (standard Twitch Bits revenue split).

## Tips & Tricks

- **Reduce visual clutter** — toggle the hammer cursor off during high-intensity gameplay and turn it back on for interactive "Just Chatting" segments where viewers enjoy bonking the screen.
- **Promote the mini-games** — let your chat know they can spend Bits to pop bubbles live on stream. The bubble-popping creates a fun, chaotic moment that works well as a reward for Bit donations.
- **Sound mix** — balance the bonk volume slider against your game audio and microphone. A moderate setting (around 40–50) keeps the interaction satisfying without overpowering your commentary.
- **Bits revenue** — since you keep 80 % of Bits spent on the mini-games, consider featuring CEOBONK as a fun, low-barrier way for viewers to support the channel while getting instant interactive feedback.

## Alternatives

- **[Viewer Attack](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/...)** (by Dried Beans) — viewers can click the stream to trigger attack animations and effects on screen. Choose Viewer Attack if you want combat-themed interactions rather than a casual hammer bonk.
- **[FIRESTARTER](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/...)** (by Hammertime Studio — same developer as CEOBONK) — viewers ignite the stream with fire effects. A great companion to CEOBONK if you want a variety of interactive overlays from the same studio.
- **[Crowd Control](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/...)** — viewers can influence your gameplay directly by spending Bits or channel points to trigger in-game events (spawn enemies, heal, etc.). Choose Crowd Control if you want deep game-specific interactivity rather than a universal overlay bonk.
- **[Sound Alerts](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/...)** — viewers spend Bits to play short sound clips on stream. Choose Sound Alerts if you prioritise audio-based interaction over visual cursor play.

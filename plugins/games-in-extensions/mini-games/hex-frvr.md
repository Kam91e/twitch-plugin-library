# Hex FRVR

> A relaxing hexagonal puzzle game for your stream where viewers place blocks on a hex grid to clear lines — no time limit, no stress, just satisfying gameplay.

![Hex FRVR gameplay showing the hexagonal grid with block pieces](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMzc5NzYvMzM2ODg5LnBuZw==/original/OK%2FWmX.png)
![Hex FRVR puzzle board with active blocks and score display](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMzc5NzYvMzM2ODkwLnBuZw==/original/oR8lYG.png)

## Quick Start

1. Go to the [Hex FRVR Twitch Extension page](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/z6ydbbfsqw6xpjytevnmzq5pff2qw8-0.0.1)
2. Click **Install** to add the extension to your channel
3. In the Extension Manager, activate Hex FRVR and assign it to a **Panel** or **Component** slot
4. Open your channel page — the game panel appears below your stream
5. Viewers can start playing immediately: just drag and drop hexagonal blocks onto the board

No setup, no configuration, no OBS browser source needed. The game runs entirely inside Twitch's extension panel.

## Features

- **Hexagonal puzzle board** — A unique hex-grid twist on the classic block-puzzle formula (no squares here!)
- **Drag-and-drop gameplay** — Place hexagonal block pieces onto the board with simple click-and-drag controls
- **3-direction line clearing** — Complete full lines in any of three primary directions (horizontal and two diagonals)
- **Combo bonuses** — Clear multiple lines at the same time to earn bonus points and satisfying chain reactions
- **No time limit** — Relaxed, casual play at the viewer's own pace; no timers, no pressure
- **Endless replayability** — No levels, no lives, no game-over screen — play as long as you want
- **10+ unlockable visual themes** — Unlock new themes (neon, wooden, and more) by earning coins through play
- **Score tracking** — Personal high scores encourage replay and competition among viewers
- **Mouse and touch support** — Works on desktop and mobile devices
- **AFK-friendly** — Viewers can keep playing even when you're away from stream (great for BRB screens or intermissions)

## Configuration

Hex FRVR has minimal configuration — it's designed to work out of the box. Settings are managed through the Twitch Extension dashboard.

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---------|------|---------|-------------|
| Theme | selection | `classic` | Visual appearance of the board and blocks. Unlock themes by earning in-game coins from play. |

### Theme Selection

Themes are unlocked progressively as viewers earn coins by playing the game. Available themes include:

- **Neon** — Bright glowing blocks on a dark background
- **Wooden** — Natural wood-textured pieces
- **Classic** — The default colourful theme
- Additional themes unlocked through gameplay

Themes can be changed at any time from the game's settings menu within the extension panel.

### Note on Streamer Configuration

Hex FRVR is a viewer-facing game with no streamer overlay or OBS integration. All interaction happens inside the Twitch panel or component slot. There is no streamer dashboard beyond the basic install and slot assignment.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Check all 3 pieces first** — Before placing anything, look at all three available hexagonal blocks. Choosing the right order to place them is the key to maximising your score.
- **Set up multi-line clears** — The real points come from combos. Arrange your blocks so that placing the final piece clears two or three lines at once.
- **Use the edges strategically** — Place pieces along the board's edges and corners to leave the centre open for more flexible arrangements.
- **Keep the board open** — Avoid filling one area too densely. A spread-out board gives you more options for fitting awkward-shaped pieces.
- **No rush** — Since there's no time limit, take a moment to think through your placement. A few seconds of planning can save many turns of recovery.
- **Unlock themes as a viewer goal** — Encourage viewers to keep playing by tying theme unlocks to channel milestones or viewer goals.
- **BRB engagement** — Leave Hex FRVR running during intermissions. Viewers will happily play while you're away, keeping the chat active.
- **Race for high scores** — Challenge your community to beat your personal high score or run a monthly high-score contest with a shoutout reward.

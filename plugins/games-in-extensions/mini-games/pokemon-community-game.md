# Pokémon Community Game

> A free-to-play Twitch Extension that lets your chat catch, trade, and battle Pokémon in real time while watching your stream.

## Quick Start

Get your community playing Pokémon in under two minutes.

1. Go to the [Twitch Extension page](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/pm0qkv9g4h87t5y6lg329oam8j7ze9) and click **Install**.
2. Set the extension as a **Panel** extension — Panel 1 is recommended so viewers see it immediately.
3. Make the **Pokémon Community Game** bot a moderator in your chat. This is required for the configuration commands to work.
4. Type `!pokestreamer on` in your chat to enable Pokémon spawns.
5. Tell your viewers to type `!Pokestart` to begin their adventure — they will receive a starter Pokémon and Pokéballs.
6. Every 15 minutes a wild Pokémon will appear in chat. Viewers type `!Pokecatch <ball type>` to try catching it.

> **Note:** If the configure button does not respond, try a hard refresh (Ctrl+F5) or open an incognito/private window.

## Features

- **Catching Pokémon** — A wild Pokémon spawns in chat every 15 minutes. Viewers type `!Pokecatch` (with a ball type) to attempt catching it.
- **Trading & Wonder Trade** — Viewers can trade Pokémon with each other (3-hour cooldown) or use Wonder Trade for a random swap (instant with a Wonder Pass item).
- **Player vs Player Battles** — Ranked and unranked battles. Pokémon scale to level 50 automatically. Ranked mode requires at least one Pokémon with 500+ base stats.
- **Pokédex (898 total)** — 734 Pokémon spawn in the wild. The remaining 164 (legendaries, mythicals, starters) are obtained through missions, tournaments, and special rewards.
- **Shiny Pokémon** — ~1/1250 base shiny rate. Cherish Balls increase shiny odds by 10x. A Shiny Charm is earned by completing the spawnable Pokédex.
- **Weekly Missions** — 5 missions per week that reward rare items and exclusive Pokémon.
- **Community Tournaments** — Regular events with unique rule sets that award rare and shiny Pokémon plus Battle Coins.
- **Pokedaily** — A daily reward accessible via the Discord bot (`!Pokedaily` command).
- **Raid Events** — Collaborative community raid events triggered with `!Pokeraid`.
- **Evolutions** — Pokémon evolve using level requirements, evolution stones, and Pokédex entry criteria.
- **Shop System** — 30+ Pokéball types, potions, revives, stat-changing items, team enhancers, and Wonder Passes.
- **In-Extension UI** — A full panel interface for viewing your Pokémon, managing teams, battling, and checking mission progress.

## Configuration

### Installation & Streamer Commands

Once the extension is installed and the bot is modded, these chat commands control the game on your channel:

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `!pokestreamer on` | Enable Pokémon spawns in your chat |
| `!pokestreamer off` | Temporarily disable spawns |
| `!pokestreamer remove-bot` | Permanently remove the bot from your channel |

### Viewer Chat Commands

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `!Pokestart` | Start the game — receive a starter Pokémon and Pokéballs |
| `!Pokebuddy` | Show your buddy Pokémon and its IVs |
| `!Pokepass` / `!Pokebank` | Check your PokéDollar balance and battle stats |
| `!Pokecatch <ball type>` | Attempt to catch the currently spawned Pokémon |
| `!Pokeshop <item> <quantity>` | Purchase items from the shop |
| `!Pokeraid` | Show current raid event progress |
| `!Pokedex <generation/type>` | View your Pokédex completion by generation or type |
| `!Pokedaily` | Claim your daily reward (requires linking Discord) |
| `!Pokecheck <Pokémon/ID>` | Check if a specific Pokémon has already been caught |
| `!Pokegift` / `!Pokegift rare` | Open a gift present |
| `!Pokeloyalty` | Check your loyalty points in the current stream |
| `!recent <Pokémon>` | Check when a specific Pokémon last spawned |
| `!pokemon <Pokémon>` | View a Pokémon's tier, base stats, and type effectiveness |
| `!move <move>` | Get detailed information about a specific move |

### Important Notes

- Subscribers earn **50% more PokéDollars** than regular viewers.
- The first channel you throw a Pokéball in is the only one that counts per spawn — you cannot catch the same spawn in multiple streams.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Ball selection matters** — Premier Balls come free when you buy 10+ of any Pokéball. Timer Balls get better catch rates the longer a Pokémon has been spawned. Quick Balls work best immediately after a spawn. Heavy Balls are more effective on heavy Pokémon; Feather Balls on light ones.
- **Wonder Trade odds** — Wonder Trade has a 3% chance of receiving a higher-tier Pokémon and a 1% chance of receiving a top-tier Pokémon. Use a Wonder Pass item to bypass the cooldown.
- **Shiny hunting** — Cherish Balls boost shiny odds by 10x. Save them for rare spawns you really want to hunt.
- **Sell wisely** — Selling items in the shop refunds half their purchase price. Factor this in when stocking up.
- **No macros** — Automated macros and bots used to farm PokéDollars are forbidden and can result in being banned from the game.
- **Weekly missions** — Complete all 5 weekly missions every week. They reward rare items and exclusive Pokémon unobtainable anywhere else.
- **Community Discord** — Join the [PCG Discord](https://discord.gg/BvaNvMD) for Pokedaily rewards, tournament announcements, raid events, and trade coordination.
- **Evolutions** — Check evolution requirements carefully. Some evolutions need specific evolution stones, level thresholds, or Pokédex completion milestones.

## Alternatives

- [Spotlite](../games-in-extensions/mini-games/spotlite.md) — A chat-based discovery and interaction game. Choose Spotlite if you want a lightweight, non-collectible social game for your chat rather than a full Pokémon RPG experience.
- **Streamlabs Chat Game** (Twitch Extension) — A simpler chat-based RPG with quests and battles. Good for variety streamers who want a quick game without the depth of 898 Pokémon.
- **Akinator** (Twitch Extension) — A chat-based guessing game where viewers try to stump the genie character. A lighter alternative if you want occasional interactive fun without persistent progression systems.

# Live Bingo

> Host live games of Bingo on your Twitch channel — viewers play along on interactive bingo cards, buy extra cards with Bits, and trigger classic bingo sound effects in real time.

## Quick Start

1. Go to your [Twitch Creator Dashboard](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/) and navigate to **Extensions > Discovery**
2. Search for **"Live Bingo"** and click **Install**
3. Once installed, open the extension in your channel dashboard — the Live Bingo configuration panel will appear
4. **Select a Bingo mode** and **choose a theme** (Classic, Pokemon, Anime, and more)
5. **Set the Bits price** viewers must pay to buy extra bingo cards (defaults are provided)
6. Click **"Create Bingo"** to start a game — bingo cards appear as an overlay on your stream
7. Viewers see their bingo cards in the extension panel and can purchase additional cards using Bits
8. Call out numbers during your stream — viewers mark their cards and a winner is declared when someone gets Bingo!
9. Use the **Live Configuration** view during your stream for quick access to game controls

## Features

- **Live Bingo games** — Host real-time bingo sessions and let viewers play along on their own cards
- **Bits integration** — Viewers spend Bits to buy extra bingo cards; streamers earn 80% of Bits revenue
- **Sound alerts** — Viewers can trigger classic bingo sound effects (BINGO call, daub, win jingle) with Bits
- **Subscriber-exclusive games** — Gift free bingo cards to subscribers and run private games or giveaways
- **Multiple themes** — Choose from Classic, Pokemon, Anime, Food, Animals, Hearthstone, and League of Legends themes
- **Custom themes** — Create your own bingo themes with custom images and layouts
- **European & American rules** — Supports both 75-ball (American) and 90-ball (European) bingo formats
- **Stream Together support** — Co-host bingo games with other streamers using Twitch's Stream Together feature
- **Chat permission settings** — Control who can participate (everyone, subscribers only, or by moderator approval)
- **Cross-platform** — Available as Component, Overlay, and Mobile extension types
- **Proven scale** — Used by 10K+ active streamers with 10M+ unique viewers and 10M+ Bits spent

## Configuration

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---------|------|---------|-------------|
| Bingo Mode | dropdown | `Auto` | Game mode — `Auto` (computer calls numbers), `Manual` (you call numbers), or `Sub Only` |
| Theme | dropdown | `Classic` | Visual theme for bingo cards: Classic, Pokemon, Anime, Food, Animals, Hearthstone, League of Legends, or Custom |
| Bits per Card | number | `10` | Number of Bits a viewer must spend to buy one additional bingo card |
| Free Cards per Viewer | number | `1` | Number of free bingo cards each viewer receives when a game starts |
| Subscriber Bonus Cards | number | `1` | Extra free cards automatically granted to subscribers |
| Max Cards per Viewer | number | `5` | Maximum bingo cards any single viewer can hold simultaneously |
| Rules Format | dropdown | `American (75-ball)` | Bingo rule set — `American (75-ball)` or `European (90-ball)` |
| Sound Effects | toggle | `On` | Enable or disable Bits-triggered sound effects during gameplay |
| Sound Price | number | `5` | Bits cost for viewers to trigger a bingo sound effect |
| Chat Permissions | dropdown | `Everyone` | Who can join: `Everyone`, `Subscribers Only`, or `Mods Only` |
| Card Auto-Daub | toggle | `Off` | Automatically mark called numbers on viewer cards (off = viewers daub manually) |
| Stream Together Mode | toggle | `Off` | Enable co-op bingo with other streamers using Stream Together |

### Bingo Mode

Choose between **Auto** (the extension calls numbers automatically on a configurable timer), **Manual** (you call numbers at your own pace), or **Sub Only** (only subscribers can play). Auto mode is best for fast-paced, hands-off sessions. Manual mode gives you control over pacing and lets you build suspense.

### Bits Pricing

Bits spent on bingo cards and sound effects are split **80% to the streamer / 20% to the developer**. Set your Bits-per-card price based on your community's spending habits — lower prices encourage participation, higher prices create exclusivity for giveaways and special events.

### Custom Themes

You can create your own bingo themes by uploading custom images for the card background, number tiles, and the free space. Theme images are managed through the Live Bingo dashboard under the "Custom Themes" tab. Share your custom themes with other streamers in the [Live Bingo Discord](https://discord.gg/U9DdwM6).

## Tips & Tricks

- **Themed bingo nights** — Pair a custom bingo theme with your stream's topic. For example, use the Food theme during a cooking stream or the Anime theme during an anime watch-along
- **Giveaway events** — Run subscriber-exclusive bingo games as a loyalty perk. Gift free cards to subs and give away channel merchandise or game keys to winners
- **Build hype with Bits** — Set a moderate Bits price for extra cards ($0.50–$1.00 equivalent) and let viewers know that 80% of the Bits come back to you. It's a fun way to support the channel while playing
- **Stream Together co-op** — Co-host bingo with another streamer using Stream Together. Cross-promote to both communities and alternate who calls numbers each round
- **Combine with channel points** — Use Twitch's built-in channel point redemptions to award free bingo cards or grant viewers an extra card without spending Bits
- **Pacing matters** — In Manual mode, call numbers slowly for chat-heavy streams so viewers don't miss a number. In Auto mode, adjust the call speed to match your stream's energy
- **Sound effects as engagement** — Encourage viewers to trigger sound alerts with Bits — the classic "BINGO!" call creates a great hype moment when someone wins
- **Custom themes for special occasions** — Create a custom bingo theme for your birthday stream, subathon, or holiday event. It's easy to swap themes between games

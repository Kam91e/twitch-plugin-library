# Twenty Forty-Eight

> A playable 2048 sliding-tile puzzle game embedded as a Twitch panel — viewers can merge tiles and chase the 2048 tile directly on your channel page.

![Twenty Forty-Eight Icon](https://xlrjj12covnq8sqwpyj080ird3hqw2.ext-twitch.tv/xlrjj12covnq8sqwpyj080ird3hqw2/0.0.2/323b86e36aaa45fbb6277a85d3839e16/internal/icon/170/483/636722170263287825.png)

## Quick Start

Get the puzzle running on your channel in under one minute:

1. Go to the [Twitch Extension Dashboard](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/xlrjj12covnq8sqwpyj080ird3hqw2-0.0.2) — or search for "Twenty Forty-Eight" in the **Discover** tab.
2. Click **Install**.
3. Switch to the **My Extensions** tab.
4. Find Twenty Forty-Eight in your installed list and click **Activate**.
5. Assign it to a **Panel** slot (Panel 1, 2, or 3).
6. Visit your channel — the game appears below the video player. Viewers can start playing immediately with no additional setup.

That's it. No configuration, no viewer installation, no channel points setup required.

## Features

- **Classic 2048 gameplay** — swipe or use arrow keys to slide numbered tiles. Merge identical tiles to double their value. Reach 2048 to win.
- **Panel-based extension** — sits in a Twitch panel below the stream video, always accessible to viewers.
- **Zero setup for viewers** — the game loads automatically when someone visits your channel. No clicks, no permissions, no installs.
- **Lightweight port** — built as a quick HTML/JS port of the original 2048 game. Runs smoothly in the Twitch extension iframe.
- **Free** — no cost to install or use.

## Configuration

Twenty Forty-Eight is a straightforward port of the original 2048 game and offers **no configurable settings**. The game runs with default tile mechanics and no adjustable parameters (grid size, tile speed, scoring rules, or visual themes).

What you see is what you get — a clean, minimal puzzle that works the moment you activate it in a panel slot.

## Tips & Tricks

While there are no hidden settings to tweak, you can share these 2048 strategies with your viewers to keep them engaged:

- **Corner strategy** — pick a corner (e.g., bottom-right) and always push your highest tile there. Keep it anchored by only moving in two directions (down and right, or down and left). This prevents your largest tile from getting stuck in the middle.
- **Plan two moves ahead** — before sliding, look at what the next tile will be and where it will land. Avoid moves that create isolated empty spaces in the middle of the board.
- **Never mash randomly** — rushed moves fill the board faster. Encourage your chat to slow down and think. A slower, deliberate play style consistently yields higher scores.
- **Keep the board tight** — try to keep tiles of similar values close together. If you have a 64 next to an 8 and a 64 next to another 64 on the other side of the board, merge the 64s first before chasing smaller merges.
- **Use it as a stream activity** — challenge chat members to beat your high score during loading screens, BRB breaks, or as a warm-up activity before the main content starts.

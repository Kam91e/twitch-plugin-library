# Blackboard

> Allow your viewers to submit sketches directly on your Twitch stream for you to review and approve.

## Quick Start

1. Install the [Blackboard Twitch Extension](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/wigu7ps6di51n6t2xo3tghei6bkr2e-0.0.2)
2. Activate it as a **Panel** extension on your channel
3. Viewers click the panel to open a drawing canvas and submit their sketches
4. Open the extension pop-out from your Creator Dashboard to review pending sketches
5. Approve or deny submissions — approved sketches appear on the panel for everyone to see

## Features

- **Viewer sketching** — Viewers draw directly in their browser and submit to your stream
- **Approval queue** — Incoming sketches land in a Pending tab for you to review before they go public
- **Panel display** — Approved sketches are shown on your channel's panel for all visitors to see
- **Free to use** — No Bits required, no paid tiers — anyone can submit a sketch
- **Simple moderation** — Full control over what gets published to your channel

## Configuration

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---------|------|---------|-------------|
| Approval required | toggle | `true` | Whether new sketches must be approved before appearing publicly |

The extension is managed entirely through the Twitch Extension pop-out in your Creator Dashboard. There you can review pending sketches, approve or deny them, and manage the display.

### Approval Workflow

Sketches submitted by viewers appear in a **Pending** tab. You can review each sketch and choose to **Approve** (makes it visible on your panel) or **Deny** (removes it). There is no limit on the number of pending sketches.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Encourage participation during lulls:** Ask chat to draw something while you're setting up a new game or taking a water break
- **Use as an icebreaker:** Start your stream by asking viewers to sketch their predictions for the day's content
- **Combine with channel point rewards:** Let viewers know that approved sketches stay visible — treat it like a digital art wall for your community
- **Check regularly:** Sketches stay in the Pending tab until reviewed; check between games or during breaks
- **Known limitation:** Some users have reported that submitted sketches do not always appear in the Pending tab — test the workflow before relying on it during a live stream

## Alternatives

- [Stream Stickers](../viewer-engagement/interactive-overlays/stream-stickers.md) — viewers place pre-made stickers on stream using Bits instead of drawing freeform sketches
- [Stream Avatars](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/...) — viewers control small characters on screen for a different interactive experience

# Dragon Pets

> Virtual dragon pets for streamers and their communities — viewers can use Bits or Channel Points to feed them, breed them, and battle them.

![Dragon Pets social image](https://www.dragon-pets.com/social-image.png)

## Quick Start

Get Dragon Pets up and running on your stream in a few minutes.

1. **Install the extension** from the [Twitch Extension Dashboard](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/7efzkm8kozkgqcsqgq6y97zum6pl2v). Click **Install** and select your channel.
2. **Activate it** in your Creator Dashboard under **Extensions > My Extensions**. Choose a slot type:
   - **Panel** — displays the dragon in your offline/about panel
   - **Overlay** — shows the dragon on your live stream
   - **Component** — places the dragon alongside other stream elements
3. **Customise your dragon** by opening the extension configuration panel. Pick a name, colour, and initial appearance.
4. **Let viewers interact!** Once live, viewers can use **Channel Points** (free) or **Bits** (paid) to:
   - Feed the dragon
   - Start a battle
   - Breed dragons together
   - Play text-to-speech messages
5. *(Optional)* Add the **browser source URL** in OBS for a persistent overlay that stays visible at all times. See the [YouTube setup tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A6QOaGkAwBA) for details.
6. *(Optional)* Add the **DP Bot** to your Discord server for cross-platform integration.

## Features

- **Virtual dragon pet** — a one-of-a-kind dragon that lives on your stream. Each dragon is 100% unique and inherits visual traits from its parents.
- **Viewer interactions via Bits & Channel Points** — viewers can feed your dragon, initiate battles, breed their own dragons, and play TTS messages. Supports both free (Channel Points) and paid (Bits) interaction.
- **Breeding system** — over 40 million unique dragon combinations. Offspring inherit visual traits from both parents, creating genuinely unique pets.
- **Battle system** — viewers can challenge each other's dragons to battles directly on stream.
- **Event reactions** — your dragon reacts when viewers subscribe, cheer Bits, or follow the channel.
- **Item Shop** — cosmetic items to dress up and customise your dragon. Monetisation split: 80% to streamer, 20% to developer.
- **Emote Maker** — a [free tool](https://www.dragon-pets.com/emotes) that turns your pet dragons into channel emotes for Twitch.
- **No OBS required** — works out of the box as a Twitch Extension. An optional browser source URL is available for persistent overlay use.
- **Mobile support** — fully functional inside the Twitch mobile app.
- **Discord Bot (DP Bot)** — integrate your dragon pet community across Twitch and Discord.

## Configuration

Dragon Pets is configured through the extension settings panel in your Twitch Creator Dashboard. Below are the main configurable areas:

| Area | Options | Description |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Dragon Appearance | Name, colour, visual traits | Set your dragon's initial look and identity |
| Interaction Costs | Channel Points / Bits amounts | Configure how many Channel Points or Bits each interaction costs (feeding, battling, breeding, TTS) |
| Event Reactions | Sub, Cheer, Follow toggles | Enable or disable dragon reactions for each type of viewer event |
| TTS Settings | Toggle on/off, volume | Control text-to-speech messages played by viewers via Bits or Channel Points |
| Overlay Position | Screen region presets | Choose where the dragon appears on your stream overlay (e.g., top-left, bottom-right) |

### Dragon Appearance
In the configuration panel you can name your dragon and choose its base colour and visual traits. More cosmetic options become available through the Item Shop as your community interacts.

### Interaction Costs
Each viewer action (feed, battle, breed, TTS) has a configurable cost in both Channel Points and Bits. You can set different prices to encourage or discourage specific behaviours. The Bits revenue split is 80% to you, 20% to the developer.

### Event Reactions
Toggle whether your dragon reacts (e.g., flies across the screen, breathes fire, does a happy animation) when a viewer subscribes, cheers Bits, or follows. You can turn individual event types on or off independently.

### Overlay Position
Choose where the dragon is anchored on screen. Preset options typically include corners and edges. This setting is only relevant when using the overlay or component slot types.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Persistent overlay in OBS:** Install the extension normally, then also add the browser source URL as a source in OBS. This keeps the dragon visible even when the extension component might hide. See the [setup tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A6QOaGkAwBA) for the exact URL.
- **Encourage breeding with lower Channel Point costs:** Set breeding costs lower than battle costs to encourage viewers to breed new dragons — this grows the community and creates more unique pets for everyone.
- **Use the Emote Maker for channel rewards:** Turn popular viewer-bred dragons into custom emotes using the [free Emote Maker](https://www.dragon-pets.com/emotes). Use these as Channel Point redemption rewards or subscriber perks.
- **Cross-platform community with DP Bot:** Add the Discord bot to your server so your community can interact with dragons even when you're not live. This keeps engagement going between streams.
- **Promote the Item Shop during slow moments:** When chat is quiet, remind viewers they can buy cosmetics for your dragon in the Item Shop — it's a natural way to generate Bits revenue.
- **Combine with Streamer Tools alerts:** Dragon Pets pairs well with alert extensions in the [Streamer Tools](/plugins/streamer-tools/) category. The dragon's event reactions complement rather than replace traditional follower/sub alert overlays.

## Alternatives

- [Stream Familiars](https://www.streamfamiliars.com/) — a similar virtual pet platform with a fantasy creature theme. Choose Stream Familiars if you prefer a different art style or creature type (non-dragon pets).
- [Kappamon](https://kappamon.com/) — another virtual pet extension for Twitch with a monster-collecting focus. Consider Kappamon if you want a catch-and-collect mechanic similar to Pokémon-style gameplay rather than dragon breeding and battles.

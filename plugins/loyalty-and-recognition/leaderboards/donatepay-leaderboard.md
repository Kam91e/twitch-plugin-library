# Donatepay Leaderboard

> Show off your top supporters across three categories — donations, Bits, and subscriptions — with an auto-updating panel from DonatePay.

## Quick Start

1. Have a **DonatePay account** (register at [donatepay.ru](https://donatepay.ru/)) and connect your Twitch channel.
2. Go to the Twitch Extension dashboard link above and click **Install**.
3. Go to **My Extensions**, find Donatepay Leaderboard, and click **Activate**.
4. Assign it to a **Panel slot** (Panel 1, 2, or 3).
5. The leaderboard populates automatically over time based on your channel's donation, Bits, and subscription data.

> **Note:** This extension is currently in **HIDDEN** state on Twitch and may not appear in public search results. Existing installations continue to work.

## Features

- **Three leaderboard categories:** Top donors (by total donation amount), top Bits contributors, and top subscribers (by longest continuous subscription).
- **Auto-updating:** Leaderboard refreshes every 30 minutes.
- **Panel display:** Appears in your channel's About section on desktop.
- **Part of DonatePay ecosystem:** Integrates directly with your DonatePay donation processing.

## Configuration

The extension has minimal configuration — install it and it pulls data automatically from DonatePay's systems. The leaderboard populates gradually, with the subscriber list filling over approximately one month as existing viewers re-subscribe.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Social proof:** The leaderboard encourages more donations by showing top supporters publicly — viewers naturally want to see their name on the list.
- **Auto-updates:** Once installed, the extension updates automatically — no manual re-installation needed.

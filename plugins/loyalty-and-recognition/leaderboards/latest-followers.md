# Latest Followers

> A Twitch panel extension that displays a live list of your newest followers and supporters, with weekly, monthly, and all-time leaderboards — powered by OWN3D Pro and Casperr.

## Quick Start

1. Install the [Latest Followers Twitch Extension](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/wb3bnc8np7tdnxj2sfbk0aa3vklulo)
2. Activate it as a **Panel** on your channel
3. Click **Configure** and authorise OWN3D Pro to read your follower list
4. Choose a free skin or keep the default clean design
5. That's it — your newest followers appear automatically

## Features

- **Live follower list** — Displays a real-time list of your most recent followers
- **Session follower counter** — Shows how many new followers you've gained during the current stream
- **Subscriber and donor highlights** — Upgrade to Widget Pass or Stream Pass to showcase subscribers, donors, and Cheers
- **Weekly, monthly, and all-time leaderboards** — Long-time fans and top supporters get recognised
- **Bits-enabled features** — Viewers can use Bits to share their follow age and celebrate milestones in chat
- **Premium skins and designs** — Unlock exclusive panel styles with Widget Pass or Stream Pass
- **Free core version** — No cost for basic functionality, works with any Twitch account
- **No affiliate requirement** — Works for all streamers, not just Affiliates and Partners

## Configuration

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---------|------|---------|-------------|
| Follower count display | number | 10 | How many recent followers to show |
| Skin/design | dropdown | default | Choose from free or premium panel designs |
| Show subscriber highlights | boolean | `false` | Requires Widget Pass or Stream Pass |
| Show donor highlights | boolean | `false` | Requires Widget Pass or Stream Pass |
| Show Bits cheers | boolean | `false` | Requires Widget Pass or Stream Pass |
| Leaderboard period | dropdown | `all-time` | Weekly, monthly, or all-time leaderboard view |

Configuration is managed through the [OWN3D Pro Dashboard](https://www.own3d.pro/dashboard/extensions/).

### Widget Pass vs Stream Pass

The core extension is completely free. Premium features like subscriber highlights, donor displays, and custom designs require either:

- **Widget Pass** — Unlocks premium skins and subscriber/donor display options
- **Stream Pass** — Full access to all premium features including custom designs

Both are part of the OWN3D Pro subscription ecosystem.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Combine with OWN3D alerts:** The extension pairs naturally with OWN3D Pro's alert widgets for a cohesive stream look
- **Use the Bits follow age feature:** Encourages viewers to celebrate how long they've followed — a great engagement hook
- **Premium skins for branding:** Upgrading to a paid pass lets you match the follower panel to your overlay design exactly
- **Encourage followers to interact:** The leaderboard creates friendly competition among regular viewers
- **No affiliate needed:** Unlike some monetisation extensions, this works for any channel size

# Tangia Stats

> Display a leaderboard of top Tangia interaction senders and run community challenges — recognising your most engaged viewers with Tangia REP rewards.

![Tangia Stats leaderboard](https://www.tangia.co/content/blog/tangia-leaderboards-and-challenges/images/header.png)

## Quick Start

1. **Have a Tangia account** at [app.tangia.co](https://app.tangia.co/) and set up Tangia on your channel (requires Twitch Affiliate for Bits features).
2. Install the **Tangia Twitch Extension** from your Creator Dashboard → Extensions.
3. Go to your **Tangia Dashboard** → **Settings** → **Stats tab** at [app.tangia.co/twitch/settings/stats](https://app.tangia.co/twitch/settings/stats).
4. Enable the **leaderboard** and/or **challenges**.
5. The panel will appear in your channel's About section on desktop.

## Features

- **Leaderboard display:** Shows which viewers send the most Tangia interactions (TTS, memes, soundbites, etc.) — top supporters get recognition.
- **Community challenges:** Set objectives for chat to reach; viewers earn Tangia REP from completing challenge goals.
- **Tangia REP system:** REP is based on activity over the past 30 days — consistently engaged viewers level up.
- **Dungeon raid stats:** If you use Tangia Dungeon, raid stats are automatically saved to the community leaderboard, showing top raiders, avatars, and loot.
- **Tangia+ boost:** Subscribing to Tangia+ ($9.99/month) gives double REP boost for faster leveling.

## Configuration

| Setting | Location | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Leaderboard toggle | Tangia Dashboard → Settings → Stats tab | Enable/disable the leaderboard panel |
| Challenges toggle | Tangia Dashboard → Settings → Stats tab | Enable/disable community challenges |
| Interaction pricing | Tangia Dashboard | Set Bits or channel points cost per interaction |
| Cooldown timers | Tangia Dashboard | Prevent same sound from being queued more than once per N seconds |
| Max queue depth | Tangia Dashboard | Cap the number of queued interactions |

## Tips & Tricks

- **Leaderboard + Dungeon = double engagement:** Dungeon raid stats automatically populate the leaderboard, so running raids boosts both simultaneously.
- **Consistency matters:** REP is based on the last 30 days of activity — stream consistently with Tangia to maintain your level.
- **Tangia+ doubles REP:** Subscribing to Tangia+ ($9.99/month) doubles your REP gain, so you level up twice as fast.
- **More interaction types = more data:** The leaderboard is most interesting when you offer a variety of interaction types for viewers to use.

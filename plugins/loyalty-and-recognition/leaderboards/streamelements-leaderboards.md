# StreamElements Leaderboards

> Celebrate and highlight your top supporters with multiple leaderboard panels, a loyalty store, stream schedule, and viewer location map — all powered by StreamElements.

![StreamElements Leaderboards preview](https://cdn.streamelements.com/assets/campari/leaderboard-extension/leaderboard-extension-example_new.png)
![Leaderboard panel options](https://cdn.streamelements.com/assets/campari/leaderboard-extension/leaderboard-panels_new.png)

## Quick Start

1. Go to the [StreamElements Leaderboards extension page](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/uaw3vx1k0ttq74u9b2zfvt768eebh1) on Twitch
2. Click **Install**
3. In your Creator Dashboard, go to **Extensions** > **My Extensions**
4. Find StreamElements Leaderboards and click **Activate**, assigning it to a panel slot
5. Click the **Configure** (gear) icon to customise your leaderboard panels
6. Select which leaderboard types to display: Points, Cheerers, Subbers, Chatters, Emotes, and/or Viewer Locations
7. Set your default panel and time periods (Session, Weekly, Monthly, All-Time)
8. (Optional) Set your stream schedule with days and times to show a countdown
9. Click **Save** and visit your channel to see the leaderboard in your About section

The extension requires an active StreamElements account — if you already use StreamElements for alerts or overlays, it connects automatically.

## Features

- **Multiple leaderboard panels** — display any combination of: Top Loyalty Points (All-Time & Current), Top Cheerers (Session, Weekly, Monthly, All-Time), Top Subbers (All-Time, Tier 1, 2, or 3), Top Chatters, and Top Emotes
- **Loyalty Store access** — show off your StreamElements loyalty store items and let viewers redeem them directly from the leaderboard panel
- **Stream Scheduler with Countdown** — share your weekly streaming schedule with a live countdown to your next broadcast
- **Viewer Locations** — display where your viewers are from, creating friendly competition between countries/regions
- **Customisable colours** — match the leaderboard's look to your channel's branding
- **Filter usernames** — exclude bots, moderators, or specific accounts from appearing on leaderboards

## Configuration

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---------|------|---------|-------------|
| Displayed Panels | multi-select | all enabled | Choose which leaderboard panels appear: Points, Cheerers, Subbers, Chat Stats, Store Items, Countdown, Locations |
| Default Panel | select | Points | The leaderboard tab shown first when viewers open the panel |
| Default Period | select | All-Time | Time period for the default panel (Session, Week, Month, All-Time) |
| Colors | colour picker | StreamElements theme | Customise the accent colour of the leaderboard to match your channel |
| Filter Usernames | text list | empty | Comma-separated list of usernames to exclude from leaderboards (e.g., bots) |
| Upcoming Streams | day/time picker | — | Set your streaming schedule for the countdown display |
| Time Zone | select | UTC | Your local time zone for accurate schedule display |

### Displayed Panels

The extension supports multiple panel types that viewers can switch between using tabs:

- **Points** — shows top loyalty points (Current and All-Time)
- **Cheers** — top Bit cheerers by session, week, month, or all-time
- **Subs** — top subbers (All-Time, or filtered by Tier 1, 2, or 3; or top gifters)
- **Chat Stats** — top chatters by message count
- **Emotes** — most-used emotes in your channel
- **Store Items** — displays StreamElements loyalty store items for direct redemption
- **Countdown** — shows your upcoming stream schedule with a live countdown timer
- **Locations** — shows which countries/regions your viewers are watching from

### Filtering Usernames

To keep leaderboards fair for your community, add bot accounts (StreamElements, Nightbot, etc.) and moderator accounts to the filter list. Bots accumulate watch time continuously and will dominate leaderboards if not excluded.

## Tips & Tricks

- **StreamElements platform** — this extension is one part of the larger [StreamElements ecosystem](../../../tools/streaming-platforms/stream-elements/overview.md), which also includes overlays, alerts, a chatbot, loyalty system, and sponsorship marketplace
- **Exclude bots first** — always add your chat bot's username to the filter list on first setup. Bots have 24/7 watch time and will permanently occupy the top spots otherwise
- **Brand matching** — use the Colors setting to match the leaderboard accent colour to your channel's brand for a cohesive look
- **Schedule visibility** — the countdown panel is a great default tab if you stream on a fixed schedule, as it tells new viewers exactly when you'll be live next
- **Location banter** — the Viewer Locations panel sparks fun competition between regions. Encourage viewers to recruit friends from their country to top the chart
- **Combine with StreamElements overlays** — if you use StreamElements for alerts and overlays, loyalty points earned through watching count towards these leaderboards automatically

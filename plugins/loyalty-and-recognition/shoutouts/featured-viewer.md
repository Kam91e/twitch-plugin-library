# Featured Viewer

> Let viewers spend Bits to become the featured viewer on your channel — a spotlight panel that showcases their profile, lets others follow them, and announces it in chat.

![Featured Viewer chat message](https://zero-absolu.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/extension-twitch-featured-viewer-message-envoye-dans-le-chat.jpg)

## Quick Start

1. Go to your Twitch **Creator Dashboard** → **Extensions** → **Discover**.
2. Search for "Featured Viewer" by Casperr and click **Install**.
3. Click the gear icon to **Configure** — set the Bits amount viewers must spend to become featured.
4. Go to **My Extensions**, find Featured Viewer, and click **Activate**.
5. Assign it to a **Panel slot** (Panel 1, 2, or 3).
6. Go live — viewers will see the panel below your stream and can click to become featured.

> **Note:** Bits features require Twitch Affiliate or Partner status.

## Features

- **Bits-powered spotlight:** Viewers spend Bits (amount set by you) to become the featured viewer for 10 minutes.
- **Profile display:** Shows the featured viewer's Twitch username, profile picture, and channel bio.
- **Follow button:** Other viewers can follow the featured viewer's channel directly from the panel.
- **Channel stats:** Displays total channel views and last game played.
- **Chat notification:** An automatic message is sent to chat announcing who became featured.
- **Real-time updates:** All viewers see changes instantly via Twitch PubSub.
- **Lightweight:** Uses Twitch's Configuration Service — no external servers or OBS sources needed.

## Configuration

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| Bits cost | Amount of Bits a viewer must spend to become featured (set in Configure panel) |

## Tips & Tricks

- **Cross-promotion tool:** Featured Viewer works both as a monetisation tool (Bits revenue) and a community cross-promotion tool — viewers who also stream get exposure.
- **Set a reasonable Bits price:** Too high and nobody will use it; too low and it loses its prestige. Watch how often it's claimed and adjust.
- **Mention it on stream:** Verbally encourage viewers to claim the featured spot — many won't notice the panel on their own.
- **Standard Bits revenue split:** You earn 80% of Bits spent; the developer (Casperr) earns 20%.

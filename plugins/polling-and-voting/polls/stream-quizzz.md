# Stream Quizzz

> Complex polls during your broadcast — a Twitch extension for creating complex polls and quizzes during your stream.

## Quick Start

1. Go to the [Stream Quizzz Twitch Extension page](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/cpo3o7jwh9j4xywgmoktu8uine4pwy)
2. Click **Install**
3. Navigate to the **My Extensions** tab in your Creator Dashboard
4. Click **Activate** and assign the extension to a **Panel** slot
5. Open the extension **Configuration** (gear icon) to create your poll questions, answer options, and quiz-style questions
6. Viewers can interact with the poll directly from the panel on your channel page

## Features

- **Complex polls** — Create polls with multiple options for detailed viewer feedback during your broadcast
- **Quiz-style questions** — Run trivia and quiz segments where viewers can test their knowledge
- **Panel display** — Appears as a panel on your Twitch channel page for persistent visibility
- **Mobile-compatible** — Works seamlessly on mobile devices so viewers can participate from anywhere
- **Multi-round support** — Designed for multi-round questions, ideal for gameplay integration and community decision-making

## Configuration

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---------|------|---------|-------------|
| Poll question | text | — | The question viewers will see and vote on |
| Answer options | list | — | The answer choices available to viewers |
| Quiz configuration | custom | — | Setup for quiz-style questions with correct/incorrect answers |

### Poll Question
Enter the question you want viewers to answer. Supports both simple polling and quiz-style formats.

### Answer Options
Define the list of possible answers viewers can choose from. For quiz mode, you can mark which answers are correct.

### Quiz Configuration
Configure quiz-specific settings including correct answer designation and multi-round question sequencing. This enables trivia sessions that run across multiple questions during a broadcast.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Multi-round quizzes** — Use the quiz-style configuration to run a series of questions in sequence during gameplay or community trivia segments. Perfect for "guess the game" or lore-based quiz events.
- **Community decision-making** — Run complex polls with several options to let your chat make multi-step decisions, such as choosing what game to play, what challenge to attempt, or what reward to unlock next.
- **Panel placement** — Since Stream Quizzz displays as a panel, keep it in a prominent panel slot near the top of your channel page to maximise visibility and participation.
- **Pair with chat engagement** — Announce new polls in chat using a command or a bot (e.g., Nightbot, StreamElements) to drive viewers to the panel and boost participation rates.

## Alternatives

- **[Stream Picks](./stream-picks.md)** — A polling panel extension with Bits-based voting. Now in HIDDEN status (no longer available for new installations), but documented for reference.
- **[Twitch Built-in Polls](../polling-and-voting/polls/twitch-built-in-polls.md)** — Native polling feature built into the Creator Dashboard. No extension required, uses Channel Points. Best for streamers who want a simple, zero-setup solution without installing an extension.
- **StreamAlive** — Live polls, quizzes, word clouds, and audience engagement tools that work through Twitch chat. A more feature-rich alternative with additional interactive formats beyond multiple-choice polling.
- **Chat Quiz by FrostyTools** — A Bits-earning quiz extension that lets viewers answer trivia questions and earn rewards. Good for streamers who want monetisation alongside quiz engagement.
- **Quiz Kit** — An established quiz extension on Twitch with a dedicated feature set for trivia and knowledge-based segments. A solid choice if Stream Quizzz does not meet your needs.

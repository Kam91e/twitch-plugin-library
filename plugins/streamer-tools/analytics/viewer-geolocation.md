# Viewer Geolocation

> See where your viewers are coming from in real-time — a live breakdown of viewer countries with ranking and join/leave feed modes.

## Quick Start

1. Go to the **Twitch Creator Dashboard** → **Extensions** → **Discover**
2. Search for "Viewer Geolocation" by CommanderRoot
3. Click **Install** and choose your preferred placement:
   - **Panel** (1, 2, or 3) — appears below your stream on the channel page
   - **Component** (1 or 2) — appears as an on-stream overlay widget
4. Open the extension settings by clicking the **gear icon** next to Viewer Geolocation in your installed extensions list
5. Choose your **display mode**: ranking view or live join/leave feed
6. Save and enjoy — viewer countries will populate automatically as people watch your stream

## Features

- **Real-time viewer country detection** — automatically detects the country of your current viewers without any action required from them
- **Ranking mode** — displays an ordered list of top viewer locations by country, including viewer counts for each nation
- **Live join/leave feed** — a rolling feed that shows viewers joining from or leaving specific countries in real-time
- **Weekly, monthly, and all-time leaderboards** — track your most loyal fans across different time periods to see where your long-term audience lives
- **Flag icons** — each country is displayed alongside its national flag for quick visual recognition
- **Mobile compatible** — works seamlessly on mobile devices, both in the Twitch app and mobile browser
- **Panel or Component placement** — install as a panel below your stream for a clean look, or as an on-stream overlay widget for live visibility

## Configuration

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---------|------|---------|-------------|
| Display Mode | toggle | `ranking` | Switch between **Ranking View** (ordered list of countries by viewer count) and **Live Feed** (real-time join/leave events) |
| Placement Type | selector | panel | Choose between **Panel** (below stream, channel page) or **Component** (on-stream overlay) |
| Panel Slot | selector | `1` | When installed as panel, choose slot 1, 2, or 3 |
| Component Slot | selector | `1` | When installed as component overlay, choose slot 1 or 2 |

### Display Mode

The **Display Mode** toggle switches the entire extension view. In **Ranking** mode, you and your viewers see a sorted list of countries with viewer counts — great for quickly understanding where your audience is clustered. In **Live Feed** mode, the extension shows a rolling timeline of join/leave events, giving a more dynamic sense of viewer movement throughout your stream.

### Placement

The extension can be installed in two locations, but **not simultaneously**. Choose based on your production needs:

- **Panel** — keeps your stream overlay clean. Viewers scroll down to see the data below the video player. Best for background analytics display.
- **Component** — appears directly on the stream overlay. Viewers see the data at all times. Best for interactive or geography-themed streams where location visibility adds to the experience.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Tailor content to your audience:** If you see a large viewer cluster from a specific country, consider acknowledging that region, playing music they'd enjoy, or scheduling a stream at a time that works for their time zone.
- **Schedule smart:** Use the weekly/monthly leaderboard data to identify when viewers from your top countries are most active, then schedule your streams to align with those peak hours.
- **Keep it clean with a panel:** Install as a Panel rather than a Component overlay to avoid cluttering your stream's visual space — the data lives below the fold and is still just a scroll away for curious viewers.
- **Variety streamer growth tracking:** Monitor how your geographic audience expands over time as you experiment with different game genres. A shift in viewer countries can signal a change in audience demographics tied to the content you're playing.
- **Combine with chat engagement:** Ask viewers to type their city or region in chat, then compare their responses to the geolocation data — it's a fun way to validate accuracy and spark conversation.

## Alternatives

- **StreamAlive Magic Maps** — interactive map that requires viewers to type their location in chat. Paid tool, not a native Twitch Extension. Choose if you want viewer-driven interaction rather than automatic detection.
- **Streams Charts Audience Geolocation** — third-party analytics platform with deep geographic breakdowns. Paid, external dashboard. Choose if you need historical analytics across multiple streams rather than live, in-stream data.
- **Geoloc by Polymedia** — previously available Twitch Extension for viewer location. Discontinued and no longer maintained. Viewer Geolocation by CommanderRoot is the recommended active replacement.

# !uptime & !watchtime

> Displays stream uptime and individual viewer watch time in a clean overlay, component, or panel — reducing chat spam from !uptime and !watchtime commands.

## Quick Start

1. Go to your **Twitch Creator Dashboard** → **Extensions** → **Discover**
2. Search for "!uptime & !watchtime" by Casperr
3. Click **Install**
4. Choose your **activation type**:
   - **Video Overlay** — appears on your stream (viewers see it while watching)
   - **Component** — appears as an on-stream widget attached to the video player
   - **Panel** — appears below your stream on the channel page
5. Click **Activate** to confirm your placement
6. (Optional) Open the extension **Settings** via the gear icon to adjust Bits preferences
7. If activated as an overlay or component, it will appear automatically when you go live — no further setup required

## Features

- **Stream uptime display** — shows how long the stream has been live, updated in real-time
- **Viewer watch time display** — shows how long an individual viewer has been watching the stream
- **Three placement formats** — Video Overlay, Component, or Panel to fit your stream layout
- **Mobile compatible** — works seamlessly on mobile devices in the Twitch app
- **Bits integration** — viewers can celebrate watch time milestones by spending Bits (80% streamer / 20% developer split)
- **Clean, minimal UI** — matches Twitch's native visual design language
- **Reduces chat spam** — eliminates the need for viewers to repeatedly type !uptime and !watchtime in chat by making the information always visible

## Configuration

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---------|------|---------|-------------|
| Activation Type | selector | Video Overlay | Choose between **Video Overlay**, **Component**, or **Panel** placement |
| Bits Toggle | on/off | off | Enable or disable Bits-based watch time milestone celebrations |

### Activation Type

Choose where the extension appears on your channel:

- **Video Overlay** — appears directly on the streamed video; viewers see uptime/watchtime at all times. Best for keeping info visible throughout the stream.
- **Component** — attaches as a widget to the video player frame. Slightly less intrusive than a full overlay while still being visible on screen.
- **Panel** — lives below the stream in the channel's panel section. Stays visible even when the stream is offline. Best for a clean, uncluttered stream layout.

You can only activate one instance at a time. Switch between types by deactivating and reinstalling in the desired slot.

### Bits Toggle

When enabled, viewers can spend Bits to celebrate watch time milestones (e.g., reaching 1 hour, 10 hours, etc.). The celebration appears as a special highlight or notification on the overlay. Revenue is split 80% to the streamer, 20% to the developer. Keep this disabled if you prefer a purely informational display without monetisation.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Eliminate command spam:** The primary benefit of this extension is removing the need for viewers to type !uptime and !watchtime in chat. If you regularly get 10+ viewers asking "how long has the stream been going?", this extension solves that instantly.
- **Passive Bits income:** Enable the Bits toggle to turn watch time milestones into a small but steady revenue stream. Even small celebrations add up over time, and viewers enjoy celebrating their dedication.
- **Panel placement for offline visibility:** Install as a **Panel** if you want the uptime/watchtime info to remain visible even when you're not live — great for communities that use your channel page as a hub.
- **Mobile viewers benefit too:** Since the extension supports mobile, your phone-based viewers also see uptime/watchtime without having to type commands on a cramped keyboard.
- **Pair with a chatbot for deeper commands:** Use this extension for the visual display and keep a chatbot (like Nightbot or StreamElements) for advanced commands like !watchtime [username] to check another viewer's time.

## Alternatives

- **Chatbot commands (Nightbot, StreamElements, Streamlabs Cloudbot)** — all major chatbots support built-in or custom !uptime and !watchtime commands. These work in chat only (no visual UI). Choose a chatbot if you already have one configured and prefer chat commands over a visual display.
- **Falkorie Watchtime** — a Twitch Extension focused on watch time tracking with a leaderboard. Choose Falkorie if you want competitive watch time rankings rather than a simple uptime/watchtime display.

**Note:** No other Twitch Extension currently offers the combined visual display of both uptime and individual watch time in overlay/component/panel formats. The chatbot alternatives are text-only and require the bot to be a moderator in your channel.

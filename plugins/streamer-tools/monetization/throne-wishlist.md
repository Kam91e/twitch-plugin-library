# Throne Wishlist

> A privacy-first gifting platform extension that displays your wishlist and top gifters in your Twitch About section as a panel, letting fans purchase gifts without sharing your personal address.

![Throne Wishlist OBS alert settings](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/y9r0qbf5/2526046416/fb98fd816d77eadcf881ee6f5007/Screenshot+2026-07-07+at+10_29_00%E2%80%AFAM.png)

## Quick Start

1. **Create a Throne account** at [throne.com](https://throne.com/) and sign up as a Creator.
2. **Add items to your wishlist** — paste links from any online store, choose from the Throne Gift Store (0% fees), or set up cash gift amounts.
3. **Install the extension** — go to [throne.com/profile/integrations](https://throne.com/profile/integrations), find the Twitch Extension section, and select **Set Up**. Alternatively, install from your Twitch Creator Dashboard → Extensions → search "Throne Wishlist."
4. **Activate as a panel** — assign it to Panel 1, 2, or 3 on the My Extensions tab.
5. **(Optional) Set up stream alerts** — in Throne Integrations, copy the browser-source URL and add it as a Browser Source in OBS/Streamlabs Desktop.
6. **(Optional) Connect a chatbot** — link Nightbot or StreamElements Chatbot for custom commands like `!wishlist` and `!throne`.

## Features

- **Wishlist panel display:** Your wishlist appears directly in your Twitch About section — viewers can browse and purchase without leaving Twitch.
- **Gifter leaderboard:** A leaderboard of top gifters is displayed alongside the wishlist.
- **Privacy-first:** Your delivery address and personal information are never shared with fans — Throne handles all shipping logistics.
- **Multi-store support:** Gifts can be added from any online store (Amazon, Etsy, etc.) or from Throne's partner brand catalog (0% fees).
- **Crowdfunding:** Fans can collectively fund larger items (e.g., a new graphics card or streaming setup).
- **Cash, Secret, and Surprise gifts:** Multiple gifting options including direct cash payouts, anonymous gifts, and surprise categories.
- **Gift messages:** Fans can leave messages with purchases, which you can display on stream.
- **Chargeback protection:** Throne provides chargeback protection for creators.
- **Stream alerts:** Real-time on-stream notifications when gifts are purchased (browser source in OBS).
- **Chatbot commands:** Integrates with Nightbot and StreamElements Chatbot for `!wishlist`, `!gift`, `!throne` commands.

## Configuration

| Setting | Location | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Wishlist items | [throne.com](https://throne.com/) dashboard | Add/remove items, enable crowdfunding, set cash amounts |
| Twitch extension integration | [throne.com/profile/integrations](https://throne.com/profile/integrations) | Connect/disconnect your Throne wishlist from Twitch |
| Stream alerts | Throne Integrations page | Browser-source alert URL, audio settings, custom styling |
| Chatbot commands | Throne Integrations → Chatbot | Connect Nightbot or StreamElements, customise messages |
| Collections | Throne wishlist dashboard | Group items into themed collections |
| Account verification | Throne settings | Request verified blue checkmark (~5–10 min processing) |

## Tips & Tricks

- **Crowdfund expensive items:** Enable crowdfunding on big-ticket items so multiple viewers can contribute small amounts toward a larger goal.
- **Use Surprise Gifts:** Let fans send you any gift from categories you choose — this adds discovery and makes great unboxing content.
- **Organise into Collections:** Group items ("Streaming Gear," "PC Upgrades," "Treats") to make browsing easier for fans.
- **Record thank-you videos:** When you receive a gift, record a personal thank-you video through Throne to build stronger community connections.
- **Turn gifts into content:** Do live unboxing streams — this rewards gifters and encourages others to participate.
- **Auto-tweet gifts:** Connect Twitter integration so gifts are automatically tweeted as both a thank-you and promotion.
- **Use all three Throne extensions:** Combine Throne Wishlist with Throne Leaderboard and Gear, Specs, and More for a comprehensive setup.

## Alternatives

- **[Streamlabs](streamlabs.md)** — The main Streamlabs all-in-one extension includes loyalty points and donation functionality, though not physical gifting.
- **Amazon Wishlist** — Classic wishlist option, but fans can see your name and city (less privacy).
- **Ko-fi** — Donation/tipping platform with wishlist-like features; supports subscriptions, tipping, and shop sales.
- **TreatStream** — Fans can buy food and drinks for streamers.
- **Wish Tender** — Another gifting/wishlist platform for creators with similar features.

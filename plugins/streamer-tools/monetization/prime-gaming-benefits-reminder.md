# Prime Gaming Benefits Reminder

> An official Twitch extension that reminds Prime Gaming members to use their free monthly channel subscription and claim other Prime benefits, while showing non-Prime viewers how to get a free sub.

## Quick Start

1. Go to your **Creator Dashboard** at [dashboard.twitch.tv](https://dashboard.twitch.tv)
2. Click **Extensions** in the left sidebar
3. Search for **"Prime Subscription and Loot Reminder"** (also listed as **"Prime Gaming Benefits Reminder"**)
4. Click **Install** and then **Activate**
5. The extension is a video overlay — it will appear automatically on your stream as a small Prime icon once installed. No further configuration is needed.

> **Note:** Only one video overlay extension can be active at a time. If you already have another overlay installed, you will need to deactivate it first.

## Features

- **Subscription Benefit Reminder** — detects when a viewer has a free Prime subscription available and displays a blue Prime icon prompting them to use it on your channel
- **Loot Reminder** — notifies viewers when new Twitch Prime loot (in-game content, skins, etc.) is available to claim
- **Crown Icon for Non-Prime Viewers** — viewers without Amazon Prime see a crown icon that explains how to sign up and get a free monthly subscription to any Twitch channel
- **Smart Priority System** — the "Sub Ready" icon always takes priority over loot reminders, ensuring viewers never miss their free sub
- **Auto-Hide** — the overlay disappears after 10 seconds if the viewer does not mouse over it, keeping the stream experience clean
- **Non-Intrusive Overlay** — sits as a small icon on the video player without blocking stream content

## Configuration

This extension requires minimal configuration — it works immediately after activation.

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---------|------|---------|-------------|
| Active Overlay | toggle | On | Enable or disable the video overlay component |

### Active Overlay
The only setting is the on/off toggle in your Extensions dashboard. The overlay activates automatically once installed. There are no customisation options for position, size, or timing — the icon appears in a fixed position on the video player and follows the default 10-second auto-hide behaviour.

**Important:** Twitch allows only **one active video overlay extension** at a time. If you install this extension while another overlay (e.g., a donation bar or alert widget) is active, you will be prompted to switch.

## Tips & Tricks

- **Verbally reinforce the visual reminder:** Mention the free Prime sub when you see the icon appear on your stream. Many viewers ignore overlays but respond to a direct call-to-action like _"If you have Prime, that little blue icon means you can sub for free!"_
- **Plan your overlay slot carefully:** Since only one video overlay can run at a time, make sure you are not relying on another overlay extension before installing this one. Consider whether the Prime reminder is more valuable than your current overlay.
- **The sub icon takes priority:** When a viewer has both an available Prime sub and unclaimed loot, only the sub icon appears. This is by design — the free sub is the most valuable benefit for both the viewer and the streamer.
- **Remind viewers to re-sub each month:** Prime subscriptions do not auto-renew. Viewers must manually re-subscribe every month. A periodic reminder benefits both the viewer (who may forget) and you (who earns the revenue).
- **Revenue per Prime sub:** Streamers earn approximately **$2.50 per Prime subscription** — the same as a paid Tier 1 sub. Encourage your Prime-eligible viewers to use their benefit.

## Alternatives

- [Exclusive Content for Subscribers](https://dashboard.twitch.tv/extensions/1f4d4b10-36e0-4f12-aeb5-cef2823b35e3) by Davekiss — a panel extension that lets you offer subscriber-only content. Choose this if you want to reward existing subscribers rather than encourage Prime sign-ups.
- **Viewer-side browser extensions** — various Firefox and Chrome extensions change the Subscribe button colour when a free Prime sub is available. These run on the viewer's end and have no setup for the streamer, but you cannot control when or how they appear.

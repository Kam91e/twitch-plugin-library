# Monetization

> This path covers the core revenue streams available to Twitch streamers, helping you turn your community's support into sustainable income. It explains how Bits and Subscriptions work as the foundation of direct viewer support, how Extensions can boost engagement and revenue through interactive experiences, how to manage ad scheduling and pre-roll suppression for a better viewer experience, and how to connect with brand sponsorships and leverage special monetization moments like Hype Trains and SUBtember. The five chapters provide practical best practices for balancing monetization with authentic community building.

## Details

### Bits and Subscriptions

Bits are a purchasable virtual currency viewers use to Cheer in chat, supporting streamers through messages and animated Cheermotes, while Subscriptions provide recurring monthly support with perks like custom Emotes, subscriber badges, ad-free viewing, and subscriber-only content. Twitch offers three subscription tiers, plus Gifted Subs (viewers gifting subscriptions to others) and Prime Gaming Subs (one free channel subscription included with Amazon Prime). The key to success is setting Sub and Bits goals, acknowledging support verbally and through alerts, offering meaningful bonus benefits, and never making anyone feel guilty for choosing not to subscribe or use Bits.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level1/monetization/bits-and-subscriptions/>

### Extensions

Twitch Extensions are interactive overlays and panels that integrate directly with your stream — from displaying leaderboards and game stats to letting viewers trigger sounds, send messages, or influence gameplay using Bits. Creators with Bits-enabled Extensions earn approximately 281% more Bits revenue monthly compared to those without, making Extensions a powerful monetization and engagement tool. You can activate up to six Extensions at once (three Panel, two Component, one Overlay), and popular options include Sound Alerts, Crowd Control, Tangia, and various game-specific integrations.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level1/monetization/extensions/>

### Running Ads

Strategic ad management balances revenue generation with viewer experience by scheduling planned ad breaks during natural pauses — between games, during stretch breaks, or when transitioning activities. Streamers can disable pre-roll ads (which play automatically for new viewers) by running mid-roll ads: for example, 30 seconds of ads every 10 minutes prevents pre-rolls for that period. The Ad Incentive program rewards consistent scheduling with additional revenue, and tools like snooze buttons, Picture-by-Picture ads, and the Ad Manager help you stay in control of when ads appear.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level1/monetization/running-ads/>

### Sponsorships

Sponsored Twitch streams involve a brand compensating you to feature their product, service, or content — ranging from product placements and sponsored gameplay to streamer-read ads and in-kind sponsorships. Completing your Creator Profile in the Twitch Dashboard increases your discoverability to potential sponsors, showcasing your channel's unique value in a professional format. Always use Twitch's Branded Content tool to disclose sponsored content, and never feel pressured to compromise your values or community trust for an opportunity that doesn't genuinely align with your brand.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level1/monetization/sponsorships/>

### Monetization Moments

Special monetization moments like Hype Trains, gift subscription discount promotions, and SUBtember can significantly boost your revenue and community excitement. Hype Trains trigger when there's a spike in Bits or subscriptions, challenging the community to reach level goals and rewarding all participants with Emotes. Monetized creators can run their own gift sub discount promotions at key moments (birthdays, streamaversaries, holidays), and SUBtember — Twitch's annual subscription celebration — has historically delivered up to 150% increases in gifted subs and 30% more total subscribers for participating creators.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level1/monetization/monetization-moments/>

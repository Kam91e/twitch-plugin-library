# Growing Your Community

> This path teaches streamers how to build a thriving, engaged community on Twitch through meaningful connections with viewers and fellow creators. It covers practical strategies for collaborating with other broadcasters, using raids to expand your network, rewarding loyal viewers with tools like Channel Points and custom emotes, and setting up alerts that celebrate community milestones. The path also explores hosting online and in-person events, making your content accessible to all viewers, and leveraging charity fundraising to unite your community around a cause. Together, these seven chapters give you the building blocks to grow from a solo streamer into a community leader.

## Details

### Collaborating

Collaborating with other streamers accelerates channel growth by combining audiences and creating unique shared experiences. Twitch offers several built-in tools — Stream Together, Drop-Ins (knocking on a live stream to join), and Co-Streaming — that make it easy to partner with other creators both online and in person. Beyond digital collaboration, attending events like Creator Clubs, Community MeetUps, and TwitchCon helps you build authentic relationships that translate into long-term support and cross-promotion.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level1/growing-your-community/collaborating/>

### Raiding

Raids let you send your viewers to another live channel at the end of your stream, introducing your community to a new creator and expanding your network organically. Making raiding part of your sign-off routine — browsing the same category, checking the raid browser, or asking friends for recommendations — helps build reciprocal relationships. When receiving raids, acknowledging the raider by name, welcoming new viewers, and using incoming raid settings to manage who can raid you are all key to turning a one-time visit into a lasting community member.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level1/growing-your-community/raiding/>

### Rewarding Viewers

Rewarding viewers transforms passive watchers into active community participants and encourages them to return regularly. Twitch provides a rich toolkit — Channel Points with custom redemptions, Power-ups using Bits, subscriber Emotes and Badges, Leaderboards, Sub Gifting, and subscriber-only benefits — that lets you recognize and incentivize engagement in ways that feel authentic to your channel. The key is designing rewards that enhance the stream experience without disrupting its flow, creating shared moments that strengthen community bonds.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level1/growing-your-community/rewarding-viewers/>

### Twitch Alerts

Alerts are customizable images, sounds, and text that pop up on stream to highlight viewer activities like follows, subscriptions, and Cheers. Personalizing the visual design, audio effects, and on-screen placement of alerts creates a unique, branded experience that deepens the connection between you and your audience. Streamers can also add Celebrations (animated emote effects) and integrate third-party alerts for tips, donations, and merchandise sales — but should always test alerts before going live to ensure they don't overwhelm the broadcast.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level1/growing-your-community/twitch-alerts/>

### Event Planning

Hosting well-planned events — whether online celebrations, milestone streams, or in-person Community MeetUps — drives engagement, growth, and community bonding. Successful online events combine a clear purpose, interactive elements (polls, leaderboards, alerts), and smart timing to maximize participation. Twitch-powered MeetUps in over 50 cities worldwide offer streamers the chance to network face-to-face with Ambassadors, Guild Leaders, Partners, and fellow Affiliates, fostering connections that translate into stronger online communities.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level1/growing-your-community/event-planning/>

### Increasing Accessibility

Making your content accessible ensures all community members — including those with disabilities — can enjoy and participate in your stream. Key practices include adding descriptive ALT text to images, ensuring your About section works with screen readers, providing closed captions through OBS plugins or Extensions, and narrating your actions on stream so visually impaired or passive viewers can follow along. These adjustments not only broaden your audience but demonstrate a genuine commitment to inclusivity.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level1/growing-your-community/increasing-accessibility/>

### Charity Fundraising

Twitch communities have raised hundreds of millions of dollars for charitable causes by uniting streamers and viewers around shared goals. Choosing a charity that aligns with your passions, setting realistic goals with milestones and incentives, and hyping your event through social media and on-stream promotion are all essential steps. Twitch's built-in Charity Tool (powered by PayPal Giving Fund) and third-party platforms like Tiltify make it easy to accept secure donations, while on-screen alerts, goals, overlays, and interactive elements keep your community informed and motivated throughout the fundraiser.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level1/growing-your-community/charity-fundraising/>

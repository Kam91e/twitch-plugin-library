# Creator Camp

A consolidated, source-traced reference for **Twitch Creator Camp** — Twitch's official, structured learning surface for streamers at https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/. Each path file mixes a path-level synthesis, a scannable per-chapter table, and a per-chapter TL;DR with a direct link back to the original chapter. Live Learning video sessions are excluded.

> All 52 chapters across 9 paths (43 in Level 1, 9 in Level 2) are summarized. Chapter titles, durations, and URLs are current as of the `last-reviewed` date in each file. TL;DRs are paraphrased summaries in the author's own words, not copies of the source text.

## Path Summaries

### Level 1

- [Get to Know Twitch](level1/get-to-know-twitch.md) — platform etiquette, building a support squad, and the policies that govern your channel.
- [Going Live](level1/going-live.md) — what you need to start streaming on PC, mobile, and gaming consoles, plus dashboard and manager basics.
- [Establish Your Brand](level1/establish-your-brand.md) — personal branding, engaging viewers, social media, and using analytics.
- [Growing Your Community](level1/growing-your-community.md) — collaborating, raiding, rewarding viewers, alerts, events, accessibility, and charity.
- [Content Categories on Twitch](level1/content-categories-on-twitch.md) — gaming, IRL, Just Chatting, creative, co-streams, fitness, food, music, and VTubing.
- [Monetization](level1/monetization.md) — Bits & subs, extensions, ads, sponsorships, and monetization moments.
- [Copyrights and Your Channel](level1/copyrights-and-your-channel.md) — copyright school, a glossary, and good habits to protect your channel.

### Level 2

- [Creator Sponsorships](level2/creator-sponsorships.md) — getting sponsored, understanding campaigns, running sponsored streams, brand safety, and certification.
- [Better Broadcasts](level2/better-broadcasts.md) — scenes & overlays, customizing alerts, Enhanced Broadcasting, and Dual Format.

Listed notes: Playlists, third-party scheduling, and Live Learning video sessions are outside the scope of this reference.

# Creator Sponsorships

> This path walks you through the full sponsorship journey on Twitch, from landing your first brand partnership to running and completing paid campaigns. It explains why brands choose certain creators and how a strong Creator Profile makes you more discoverable, then breaks down the different campaign types and deliverable formats you'll encounter in the Sponsorship Portal. It also covers how to run a sponsored stream day-of, what brand-safety considerations to keep in mind, and why completing Twitch's Creator Sponsorship Certification can unlock early access and better visibility with advertisers. Together, these five chapters give you everything you need to approach sponsorships professionally and protect both your relationships and your channel.

## Details

### Getting Sponsored

Brands typically choose creators based on how well a creator's audience, content, interests, and personality align with their own goals. Before you can see or accept campaign invitations, you need to sign the Sponsorship Portal Terms, and you're strongly encouraged to build out a thorough Creator Profile that tells brands who you are, what you create, and the community you represent. New sponsorship opportunities come through on-site and email notifications, so keeping your profile detailed and being open to new content formats makes it easier for brands to find and consider you.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level2/creator-sponsorships/getting-sponsored/>

### Understanding Campaigns

Twitch's Sponsorship Portal offers three campaign types: Exclusive campaigns (invite-only and limited-time offers to you), Open Invite campaigns (open to a large pool of qualifying creators, first-come first-serve, paid either per Completed View or as a fixed flat fee), and Third-Party campaigns from other services. Deliverables you may be asked to fulfill include Channel Skins, Streamer-Read Ads, discounted Sponsored Subs, or a Channel Takeover that combines all three. Before accepting, always carefully review the campaign brief, talking points, deliverables, and time window — accepting work you can't complete can impact your payout and future sponsorship opportunities.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level2/creator-sponsorships/understanding-campaigns/>

### Sponsored Streams

Before going live, add the Sponsorship Tool to your Stream Manager's Quick Actions bar, ensure your stream is in any required category, and set auto-mod to at least Level 2. On stream, activate the deliverable (such as a Channel Skin or Sponsored Subs) from the desktop-web Sponsorship Tool to start your timer, and for read ads trigger the Streamer Read Ad button before speaking so your accuracy can be recorded. After your segment passes moderation, payouts arrive alongside your normal Twitch earnings the following month, and enabling Disconnection Protection offers a 90-second safety window while the Abandon action can reset a deliverable if needed.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level2/creator-sponsorships/sponsored-streams/>

### Brand Safety

Treating every sponsorship as a professional relationship is key to building long-term credibility with advertisers. Most content on Twitch is acceptable for sponsorships, but brands are often sensitive to mature content and may include guidelines about what to avoid, so be deliberate about what you show during the sponsored segment. Watch for other brands visible in your broadcast (Exclusive campaigns typically include a one-week exclusivity window around the campaign), keep excessive swearing in check, and remember that every audio and visual element of your stream counts as part of the sponsorship.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level2/creator-sponsorships/brand-safety/>

### Sponsorship Certification

Completing Twitch's Creator Sponsorship Certification unlocks two key benefits: 12 hours of early access to select Open Invite campaigns before non-certified creators, and visibility in your Creator Profile so brands can actively search for certified partners. The certification runs through a pass/fail quiz covering the rest of the path's material, and passing is reflected on your Creator Profile so brands know you're serious about sponsorships.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level2/creator-sponsorships/sponsorship-certification/>

# Better Broadcasts

> This path helps you make your broadcasts look and perform better by focusing on the presentation and technical side of streaming. It covers designing purposeful scenes and overlays that match what's happening on stream and keep your layout clean and on-brand, plus how to customize Twitch Alerts with branded visuals and Celebrations to reward viewers. The path also introduces Enhanced Broadcasting, a feature that sends multiple video-quality variants so viewers get the best experience for their device, and Dual Format, which lets you broadcast in both horizontal and vertical simultaneously from a single stream. These four chapters give both new and experienced streamers concrete ways to elevate stream quality and reach more viewers.

## Details

### Stream Scenes & Overlays

Using a variety of intentional scenes for different moments of your stream — Starting Soon, Just Chatting, gameplay, Be Right Back, and Ending Stream — keeps content engaging and creates smooth transitions between segments. Each scene has a purpose: starting and ending scenes build anticipation or drive calls to action, while chat and be-right-back scenes give viewers something to engage with when you're away. Incorporate consistent branding across your scenes and on-screen channel data (like follower or subscriber counts and Creator Goals) to create a polished, recognizable broadcast without letting the layout get cluttered.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level2/better-broadcasts/stream-scenes-and-overlays/>

### Customizing Twitch Alerts

Twitch Alerts are a simple way to make a stream feel engaging by popping up in celebration when a viewer subscribes, cheers, follows, or raids. Customizing them with your branding — and pairing them with animated Celebrations like confetti, emotes, or fireworks — makes your channel more cohesive and professional. Adding alerts for Channel Point redemptions, sub counts, and bit contributions can incentivize participation, as viewers who redeem points are significantly more likely to keep coming back.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level2/better-broadcasts/customizing-twtitch-alerts/>

### Enhanced Broadcasting

Enhanced Broadcasting lets you send multiple video-quality variants directly from your broadcasting software, giving viewers more quality options that match their device and bandwidth. Turning it on unlocks access to 2K/1440p streaming (for Affiliates and Partners) and Dual Format, though it does add strain to your system, so review the recommended system requirements first. For Dual Format and 2K, Twitch adds server-side transcode support for Partners and select Affiliates, so your setup only needs to encode the top two variants while Twitch handles the rest.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level2/better-broadcasts/enhanced-broadcasting/>

### Dual Format

Dual Format lets you broadcast in both horizontal and vertical simultaneously from a single stream, so every viewer sees a version matched to how they're watching — a big plus since a large share of new viewers come to Twitch on mobile. Vertical layouts stand out in the mobile feed and give you both horizontal and vertical versions of your Clips and VODs. Getting started involves installing the Aitum Vertical plugin, setting up separate vertical layouts in OBS, and linking scenes, with minimum system requirements to review before broadcasting.

**Source:** <https://www.twitch.tv/creatorcamp/en/level2/better-broadcasts/dual-format/>

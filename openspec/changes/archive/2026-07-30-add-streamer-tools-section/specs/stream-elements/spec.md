## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Document StreamElements overview (overview.md)
The `overview.md` SHALL explain that StreamElements is a free all-in-one cloud-based streaming platform providing overlays, alerts, chatbot, loyalty system, sponsorship marketplace, and analytics.

#### Scenario: Reader understands the platform
- **WHEN** a reader opens `overview.md`
- **THEN** they SHALL understand it is a cloud-based platform (not a desktop app) and see its main capabilities

### Requirement: Document setup process (overview.md)
The `overview.md` SHALL explain how to create a StreamElements account, connect a Twitch account, and navigate the dashboard.

#### Scenario: Beginner can onboard
- **WHEN** a new streamer follows the setup guide
- **THEN** they SHALL be able to connect their Twitch channel to StreamElements

### Requirement: Distinguish from StreamElements Twitch Extension (overview.md)
The documentation SHALL clarify that StreamElements also has specific Twitch Extensions (e.g., StreamElements Leaderboards documented in `plugins/`) but the platform is much broader than any single extension.

#### Scenario: Reader is not confused by dual presence
- **WHEN** a reader encounters both the tool and plugin documents
- **THEN** they SHALL understand the relationship between the platform and its Twitch Extensions

### Requirement: Document overlays and alerts (overlays-and-alerts.md)
The `overlays-and-alerts.md` SHALL document the widget library, how to add overlays to OBS via Browser Source, how to customize alerts (follow, sub, raid, bits), and the visual editor.

#### Scenario: Streamer can set up overlays
- **WHEN** a streamer reads the overlays and alerts guide
- **THEN** they SHALL know how to add StreamElements overlays to OBS and customize alert appearance

### Requirement: Document chatbot and loyalty system (chatbot-and-loyalty.md)
The `chatbot-and-loyalty.md` SHALL document the chatbot command system, loyalty points, channel points integration, mini-games, giveaways, and the StreamElements marketplace.

#### Scenario: Streamer can configure chatbot
- **WHEN** a streamer reads the chatbot and loyalty guide
- **THEN** they SHALL know how to create chat commands, set up loyalty points, and run giveaways

### Requirement: Document analytics and sponsorships (analytics-and-sponsorships.md)
The `analytics-and-sponsorships.md` SHALL document stream statistics, viewer metrics, the sponsorship marketplace, and revenue tracking.

#### Scenario: Streamer can understand analytics
- **WHEN** a streamer reads the analytics guide
- **THEN** they SHALL know what metrics are tracked and how the sponsorship marketplace works

### Requirement: Include alternatives (overview.md)
The `overview.md` SHALL list alternatives: Streamlabs, OWN3D, Muxy, and explain key differences.

#### Scenario: Reader can compare platforms
- **WHEN** a reader finishes the overview
- **THEN** they SHALL know what alternatives exist and when to choose them

# Elgato — Camera Hub

> Sub-actions for controlling Elgato Camera Hub devices from Streamer.bot: the Prompter, connected webcams, and EpocCam.

Camera Hub sub-actions let you drive Elgato hardware directly from Streamer.bot actions: teleprompter control (chapters, scripts, auto-scroll, appearance), webcam settings (exposure, focus, pan/tilt, zoom, color), and EpocCam settings (AR lens, bitrate, flash). Official documentation: <https://docs.streamer.bot/api/sub-actions/elgato/camera-hub>

## Groups

- [Prompter](prompter.md) — Chapter navigation, script selection, auto-scroll and display settings for the Elgato Camera Hub Prompter.
- [Webcam](webcam.md) — Image, focus and movement settings for Elgato Camera Hub webcams.
- [EpocCam](epoccam.md) — AR lens, bitrate, camera selection and flash mode for Elgato EpocCam.

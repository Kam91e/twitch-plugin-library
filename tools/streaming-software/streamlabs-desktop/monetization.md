# Streamlabs Desktop — Monetization

> Streamlabs Desktop includes built-in monetisation tools — tipping, a merch storefront, and the Ultra subscription that unlocks additional revenue features.

## Tipping System

Streamlabs provides a complete tipping platform integrated directly into Streamlabs Desktop.

### How It Works

1. Create a Streamlabs tipping page at streamlabs.com → **Tip Page**
2. Viewers send tips through the page using PayPal, credit/debit cards, or Streamlabs wallet
3. Tips appear as alerts on your stream automatically
4. Funds are held by Streamlabs and can be withdrawn to your bank account

### Fees

- **Standard**: no monthly fee, Streamlabs takes a percentage per transaction
- **Ultra**: reduced fees, custom tip page domain, higher profit margins

### Tip Page Customisation

- Custom colours, fonts, and branding to match your stream
- Choose which payment methods to accept
- Set minimum and maximum tip amounts
- Custom preset tip amounts for quick selection
- Display recent tippers (opt-in by the tipper)

## Merch Store

Streamlabs integrates a merch storefront directly into Streamlabs Desktop.

### How It Works

1. Design merchandise (t-shirts, hoodies, mugs) through the Streamlabs merch dashboard
2. Products are printed on demand — no inventory, no upfront cost
3. Display merch in a widget on your stream or share the store link
4. Earnings are paid out through your Streamlabs tipping account

### Features

- Print-on-demand — no inventory risk
- Automatic fulfilment and shipping
- Integrated storefront widget for your stream
- Promote specific items during streams with quick links

## Tips & Tricks

- Create a dedicated "Support the Stream" scene with your tip page and merch links
- Promote your merch during natural breaks — starting soon and stream ending are perfect moments
- Use the Tip Tracker widget to visualise progress toward a goal (e.g., "Help me get a new microphone!")
- Ultra pays for itself if you stream to multiple platforms or use Collab Cam regularly
- Test your tip page flow before going live — send yourself a small tip to verify everything works

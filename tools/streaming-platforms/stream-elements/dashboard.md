# StreamElements Dashboard

> The dashboard at streamelements.com is the command centre of StreamElements — the navigation hub from which every feature of the platform is managed.

## Overview

The dashboard is where all StreamElements features are managed. After logging in with your Twitch account, the dashboard is the landing page of the platform — everything from overlays to the chatbot, loyalty, tipping, and sponsorships is reached from here. This page is the entry point for the StreamElements documentation: use the sidebar links below to jump to the dedicated guide for each feature area.

## Dashboard Page

The default view after logging in includes:

- **Top bar** — a time-range selector that adjusts the analytics shown on the page
- **Profile menu** — your account menu with an online indicator (a red ring when you are live), account switching, overlay import, language selection, dark/light theme toggle, and links to SE.Live and Ground Control
- **Quick settings** — set your stream title and game category directly from the dashboard
- **Bot presence status** — Join, Part, Mute, and Unmute buttons that control whether the chatbot is active in your chat

## Activity Feed

The Activity Feed lives on the dashboard page (it is not a separate file). It shows a live feed of events such as tips, follows, subscriptions, and more. You can filter the feed by platform and event type, replay or check the details of individual alerts, and mark notifications as read. Moderators also get ad-control options, and there is a session reset option to clear the current session's data.

## First-Setup Wizard

When setting up a new account, follow the wizard's steps in order:

1. **Connect your Twitch account** — click **Login with Twitch** and authorise the connection
2. **Activate and mod the chatbot** — make the bot join your chat and grant it moderator permissions
3. **Set up tipping** — configure PayPal or SE.Pay (Stripe) as payment methods and add the tipping panel to your channel
4. **Create or pick an overlay** — create a new overlay, choose one from the gallery, or import an existing one (see [Overlays and Alerts](overlays-and-alerts.md))
5. **Install SE.Live for OBS** — download the desktop app to stream from OBS (see [SE.Live](se-live.md))

## Tips

- The sidebar is the fastest way to reach any feature; the **Documentation** link at the bottom opens [docs.streamelements.com](https://docs.streamelements.com)
- Use the **Activity Feed** to spot missed events and replay alerts you did not see live
- The bot status buttons in the top bar let you mute or part the bot without opening the settings
- Start with the **first-setup wizard** so you do not miss important steps like tipping or overlay setup

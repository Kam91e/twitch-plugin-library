# Analytics and Sponsorships

> StreamElements provides stream analytics to track performance and a sponsorship marketplace that connects creators with brands for paid partnerships.

## Overview

Beyond overlays and chatbots, StreamElements offers two features focused on growing your stream as a channel: an **analytics dashboard** that tracks viewership, chat activity, and revenue trends; and a **sponsorship marketplace** where brands list paid campaigns that streamers can apply to join. The sponsorship marketplace is one of StreamElements' most distinctive features — it provides a direct path to monetisation beyond donations and subscriptions.

## Configuration

### Analytics Dashboard

Access analytics from the **Analytics** section of the StreamElements dashboard. Data is collected automatically once your Twitch account is connected — no additional setup is required.

**Available metrics:**

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Viewer count** | Average, peak, and concurrent viewers over time |
| **Chat activity** | Messages sent, active chatters, chat rate |
| **Followers** | Growth tracking, follower gains/losses per stream |
| **Subscribers** | New subscriptions, resubscriptions, gifted subs |
| **Bits** | Bits cheered, top bit cheerers |
| **Stream time** | Total hours streamed, average stream length |
| **Revenue** | Combined view of subscriptions, bits, and donations |

**Dashboard features:**

- **Time range filters** — view data by stream, day, week, month, or custom range
- **Comparison** — compare current period to previous period for growth tracking
- **Export** — download data as CSV for external analysis
- **Heatmaps** — see when viewer activity peaks during your streams
- **Stream summaries** — automatic recap of each stream's performance after going offline

### Sponsorship Marketplace

The sponsorship marketplace connects streamers with brands for paid campaigns. Access it from the **Sponsorships** tab in the dashboard.

**How it works:**

1. **Brands create campaigns** — companies define campaign goals, requirements, and budget
2. **Streamers browse and apply** — filter campaigns by category, payout, audience requirements
3. **Brands review and accept** — selected streamers receive campaign details
4. **Stream and promote** — complete campaign tasks during streams (play a game, use a product, mention a brand)
5. **Get paid** — payment is processed through StreamElements after campaign completion

**Campaign types:**

- **Broadcast integration** — display brand assets in your stream overlays
- **Gameplay promotion** — play and showcase a specific game
- **Social media** — post sponsored content on social channels
- **Product reviews** — use and review a product on stream

**Requirements:**

Sponsorships are available to streamers who meet minimum requirements (which vary by campaign). Common requirements include minimum average viewers, streaming frequency, and audience demographics. StreamElements vets all brands and campaigns before listing them.

## Tips

- Check the Sponsorships tab weekly — campaigns are added regularly and have limited slots
- Keep your stream's analytics healthy (consistent schedule, growing average viewers) to qualify for more campaigns
- Use stream summaries to identify what content drives the highest engagement
- Export monthly analytics for your own records or to include in media kits when applying for external sponsorships
- The sponsorship marketplace is competitive — a polished stream with active chat and consistent schedule has better chances of being selected
